---
title: What a seal proves
description: What a date on chain shows, and what it does not.
---

| It shows | It does not show |
|---|---|
| Who sealed first, by chain time | Who wrote the idea |
| That a revealed text matches its seal | That the idea is original |
| Whether a later entry reproduces it, as validators read it | Any legal right |

## Who was first is a fact

The earlier entry A must have been sealed **strictly before** the later entry B. The contract checks it when the challenge is filed and again when it is judged. The validators are never told the dates, so priority cannot leak into the label, and the model never decides who was first.

## Overlap is a judgment

Whether two texts overlap is a reading judgment, which is why the label, both texts and the prompt are all public. The judge is asked about substance, not wording, length or language, and ideas everyone in the field already uses do not count.

## Sealing someone else's idea

If you seal another person's unpublished idea first, Prior cannot tell. It proves who sealed first, not who wrote it.
