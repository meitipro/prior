## What the judge never sees

The prompt carries the two texts and nothing else: no dates, no names, no addresses, and no word about which came first. The contract always passes the **earlier** entry as TEXT 1, so a copy cannot be hidden by asking the question the other way round. A test asserts that no address, date or seal appears in the prompt.

## What validators agree on

The leader and every validator ask the same prompt and answer one label. A validator agrees only when its own label is the leader's: `mine["label"] == theirs.get("label")`, with no tolerance. The verdict is then code: SUBSTANTIAL is PRIOR, PARTIAL is RELATED, NONE is CLEARED, and the seal dates are checked in code, so who was first is a fact on chain and never the model's opinion.
