## Is it a copyright?

No. Prior records who sealed first and whether a later entry reproduces an earlier one. It gives no legal rights.

## Can others see my idea?

Not until you reveal it. The chain stores a hash, the date and your bond. Your text and salt stay in your browser as a ticket.

## What if someone sealed it first?

Then they are first on chain, whoever wrote it. Prior cannot tell, and says so on every entry.

## What if I lose my ticket?

The seal can never be revealed, because only the text and the salt reveal it. Download the ticket when you seal.

## Can I challenge before I reveal?

No. A challenge needs both entries revealed, with yours sealed strictly before the other.

## Why did the judge say PARTIAL for my reworded copy?

The label is the validators' agreement on substance, not wording. If it looks wrong, both texts and the prompt are public. The [evaluation](/docs/more/evaluation) publishes the cases where the validators and the spec disagree.

## Which network is it on?

GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997. See [Addresses](/docs/reference/addresses).

## Why did my transaction say ACCEPTED but nothing happened?

Validators can agree that a refusal is the correct result. Success is an accepted status **and** a leader execution that returned. The site shows the contract's own sentence when it refuses; see [Errors](/docs/reference/errors).
